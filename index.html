Hide & Seek Wildlands

2–3 friends · roll the dice · the winner hides in a bush · everyone else searches a huge wild world full of lions, wolves, bears and crocodiles. The hider can never be attacked.

How to play
  • Move: WASD / arrows · Run: Shift · Jump: Space · Look: mouse (click to lock) · Zoom: wheel · Map: M · Action: E
  • Phone: left thumb = joystick, right thumb = look, buttons for Run / Jump / Action. Tap the map to enlarge it.
  • Everyone rolls a die. Highest roll hides (ties re-roll).
  • The hider gets 75 s (host can change it) to get into a bush. Seekers are blindfolded at camp.
  • Seekers then have 5 minutes (adjustable). Walk up to a bush and Search it. Every minute a yellow hint circle on the map shrinks around the hider.
  • Nobody online? Press Play alone vs a bot, or Add a bot in the lobby. Bots roll, hide and seek like a real player, using the same map hints.
  • Wild animals hunt seekers. Get mauled and you are sent back to camp. The hider is never attacked.
